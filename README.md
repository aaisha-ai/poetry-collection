# Her  
The first time I saw her, she felt like light,
 Soft and warm, like the calm in night.
 she smiled, and I wondered what dreams she chased,
 What love she held, what pain she faced.
I stood and waited, day after day,
 Not knowing if she'd look my way.
 Even when she didn’t, I still stayed—
 Love, not pride, was the choice I made.
She seemed so close, yet far to reach,
 A quiet soul, with more to teach.
 When she vanished for days, I missed her face,
 That’s when I knew—it wasn’t just a place.
I read her letter, heart torn apart,
 But still found peace in her poet's heart.
 She was the song I didn’t expect,
 But every beat of it, I’ll protect.

# Him
He appeared like a question I couldn't ask,
 Stood there daily, unbothered by the task.
 Not a word, not a sound, yet something grew,
 A presence so steady, it felt like new.
His silence held more than anyone’s speech,
 He gave me the space I thought none could reach.
 I waved when I left, unsure if he’d care,
 But he always stayed, like he’d always be there.
He saw parts of me I never did,
 Pulled out the thoughts I always hid.
 His laugh, his care, his quiet way—
 All of it made me want to stay.
He made the world feel less unknown,
 Made “safe” feel something I could own.
 I never planned for love this way,
 But now he’s here, and I hope he’ll stay.
